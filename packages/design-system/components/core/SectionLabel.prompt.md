A small uppercase blue eyebrow that sits above a heading or panel title (e.g. "ASSISTANT", "VERSION OF THIS COMPOSITION"). It carries section identity in Evermore's wide-tracked, 12px label style.

```jsx
<SectionLabel>Assistant</SectionLabel>
<h2>Sally's records</h2>
```

Renders a `<p>` by default; pass `as` to change the tag. Keep the text short — one to three words.
