Small inline pill for status, category, or metadata. Tinted background plus matching text.

```jsx
<Badge variant="info">Draft</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent">Needs review</Badge>
<Badge variant="success">Applied</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral` (gray), `info` (blue — the brand default for live/active state), `accent` (amber — attention, "review this"), `success` (green), `error` (red). Keep labels to one or two words.
